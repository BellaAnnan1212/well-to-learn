## Data sources

The numbers in the Atlas come from public sources (among them WHO, UNESCO UIS, the World Bank, the UN Treaty Collection, End Corporal Punishment and GCPEA), each under its own terms. Those terms, and the attribution each source asks for, are listed on the Methodology page of the site (`site/methodology/`) and in `site/data/sources.json`. The derived JSON files in `site/data/` inherit the strictest licence among the sources they draw on: where a source is non-commercial or share-alike, that condition carries through.
