Well to Learn · a register of promises, numbers and voices

You cannot learn if you are not well.

Nearly every country has promised its young people school, and health that includes the mind. Most countries have no nationally representative measurement of children's mental health. This site keeps the register: what was promised, what is counted, and what young people say for themselves.

Three lines from the register

Every line carries a stamp for what kind of number it is, its year, and a link to the primary source. The rest of the register lives in the Atlas and on Methodology.

  • Legal

    Free, compulsory primary education is treaty law under the ICESCR (adopted 1966) and the CRC (adopted 1989); 173 and 196 states are parties, as at the date stamped on each treaty page.

    196states party to the CRC

    UN Treaty Collection · treaty pages read 3 September 2026 · treaties.un.org, chapter IV-11 (CRC, 196 parties) · chapter IV-3 (ICESCR, 173 parties)

  • Modelled

    273 million children and youth were out of school in 2024 (UNESCO Institute for Statistics and GEM Report out-of-school model, March 2026): 79 million of primary age, 64 million lower-secondary, 130 million upper-secondary; the share has been flat at about 17% since 2015.

    273million, 2024

    UNESCO Institute for Statistics and GEM Report · out-of-school model, published 26 March 2026 · world-education-blog.org/2026/03/26/the-out-of-school-population-has-risen-for-a-seventh-year-in-a-row

  • Modelled

    WHO estimates, from GBD 2021 modelling, that about one in seven (14.3%) 10 to 19-year-olds live with a mental health condition.

    about1 in 7aged 10 to 19, GBD 2021

    WHO adolescent mental health fact sheet · GBD 2021 · who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/adolescent-mental-health

From the Atlas

There is no map on this page yet, and no country card, because there is no checked data to put in one. What is settled is the key the map will be read with, so it is printed here first.

The key, before there is a map
BandColourWhat it will mean
band 1 of 5the lowest values on the layer
band 2 of 5lower than the middle
band 3 of 5the middle of the layer
band 4 of 5higher than the middle
band 5 of 5the highest values on the layer
no dataa hatch, never a colour, and never zero

The rule under each band thickens with the band, so the order survives without colour. No band carries a number here: the thresholds are published with the data they describe, never before it. No colour for modelled prevalence.
