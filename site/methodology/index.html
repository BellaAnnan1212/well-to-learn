The register · Methodology

Methodology

The rules this site keeps, written down before the data arrives, so they cannot be bent to suit a number later.

The rules

Each rule below already governs what may appear on this site. The per-source detail, with extraction dates, is added as each source lands.

  • Numbers

    No ranking and no composite score. Countries are never ordered best to worst, and no indicators are ever added together into a single figure. A map layer shows one indicator at a time, and nothing else.

  • Stamps

    Every value wears one of four stamps: measured, modelled, self-reported or legal. Measured means somebody counted it. Modelled means a model estimated it. Self-reported means a government answered a questionnaire about itself. Legal means it is the status of a treaty, not a measurement.

  • Years

    Every value carries its own year, the year that value refers to, not the year the file was downloaded. Two countries on the same map may be showing different years, and each says which.

  • Missing

    Missing is written "no data", never zero, and never quietly left out. On a map it is a hatch, which is a pattern and not a colour, so it can never be read as a low value or a high one.

  • Colour

    No colour for modelled prevalence. How many young people in a country live with a mental health condition is a modelled estimate almost everywhere, and colouring a map by it would make a model look like a count. The modelled out-of-school rate may be coloured, and is tagged as modelled wherever it appears.

  • Anonymity

    Interviewees are shown as an age band and a UN sub-region, never a town, a school or a date of birth. The age bands are 12-14, 15-17 and 18-19. A country appears only when that person said it could. Names are pseudonyms, and a drawing is generated from a seed rather than chosen to resemble anyone.

  • Consent

    Consent is recorded by month, never by day. Fifteen exact interview dates read together are an itinerary, so a profile says the month it was given and nothing finer. Consent is given knowing the page will be public and anonymous, and can be withdrawn.

  • Safety

    Safe-messaging rules apply to every page: no methods, no locations, no rankings by suicide rate, no language that makes distress sound like a wave or a plague, and help lines beside anything that might land close to home. A content note goes before a story that needs one.

  • Sources

    Every number links to the source that published it, with the date that source was read. A value that cannot be read back against its own source is not published, and a value that turns out to be wrong is corrected or removed rather than explained away.
