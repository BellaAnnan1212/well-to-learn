Voices

5 young people, interviewed by Bella, on school, their minds, and what they want changed. Every profile is anonymous: a pseudonym or no name at all, an age band, a UN sub-region, a schooling status, and a drawing that is not a likeness.

The roll call

Every voice here is a row before it is a card. The number is the profile's permanent key, so it never reorders and never ranks. Nobody on this site is ever stamped: stamps are for numbers.

  • Row p01

    Maya

    15-17 · Eastern Africa · interrupted

    Here.

    In a normal week, works in the morning, then goes to school if there is fee money; if there is not, works more.

  • Row p02

    Diego

    12-14 · Central America · irregular attendance

    Here.

    Goes to school in the mornings when he can and works with a relative in the afternoons, because it is needed at home.

  • Row p03

    Nusrat

    15-17 · Southern Asia · out of school

    Here.

    Nusrat was in school until recently and now spends most of her week at home, helping her family.

  • Row p04

    Name withheld, row p04

    12-14 · Western Africa · in school

    Here.

    They go to school and help an older brother earn money afterwards.

  • Row p05

    Name withheld, row p05

    15-17 · Western Asia · irregular

    Here.

    They came to where they live now a few years ago; a normal week is school on some days and home a lot, because their language classes clash with their regular ones and they get confused about where to be.
