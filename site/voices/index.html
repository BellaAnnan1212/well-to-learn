How a profile gets here

An interview becomes a public profile only when four things are true: the young person consented knowing the page is public and anonymous, the draft has been checked line by line for any detail that could identify them, it has been checked against safe-messaging rules, and they and Bella have both approved it. Any one of those missing, and the page is not built at all.

What a profile shows: an age band, a UN sub-region, a schooling status, and a drawing generated from a seed that was never chosen to resemble anyone. A country appears only when that person said it could. Nobody on this site is ever stamped: stamps are for numbers.
