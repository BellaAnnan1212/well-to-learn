Row p02

Diego

12-14 · Central America · irregular attendance

Here.

Anonymous by design. The name is a pseudonym and the drawing is not a likeness.

At a glance

  • Goes to school in the mornings when he can and works with a relative in the afternoons, because it is needed at home.
  • Some weeks work comes first and school does not happen at all.
  • When his mind drifts in class he gets told off for not paying attention, as if it were on purpose; he remembers the one teacher who asked why he looked tired.
  • Has not really asked for help, because he did not think there was a "help" to ask for; it felt like something he just had to manage himself or with family.
  • The relative he works with is also the person who actually helped, checking whether Diego understood the homework even while they are working.
  • Nobody has ever explained his rights to school, to help, or to be heard; he assumed working instead of school was a choice made at home, not something anyone owed him an answer for.

In their words

What they want changed

He wants school and work to stop being a fight against each other: maybe classes at a different time, or someone checking in instead of just marking him absent. What he wants readers to understand: missing school is not the same as not caring about it.
