Atlas

A world map of ten indicators, with a card for every country and a plain table carrying the same numbers for anyone who would rather not use a map.

Not published yet

The Atlas is being built. Every value will carry its year, its type and its source.

No map and no country card appear here until each value has been read back against the source that published it. A number that has not been checked is not shown at all, and a country with nothing to show is never shown as a zero.

How each value is checked, and what each stamp means: Methodology

What will be here

Ten indicators in the first version, with no composite score and no ranking of any country against another:

  • Suicide rate, ages 10 to 19
  • Age-standardised suicide rate, with its low and high bounds
  • Psychiatrists per 100,000 people
  • Out-of-school rate, lower secondary age
  • Completion rate, lower secondary
  • Learning poverty at age 10
  • Whether corporal punishment is prohibited in schools
  • Treaty status: CRC, ICESCR, CRPD, and the CRC complaints protocol
  • Whether the country is profiled in Education under Attack
  • The WHO Mental Health Atlas 2024 country block: policy, school programme, spending share

Each country card will read in one order: who lives there, young people, care, school, promises, conflict, and voices from that region. Each field will name its source and the date it was read.

Every value will wear one of four stamps: measured, modelled, self-reported or legal. Missing is written "no data", never zero. No colour is used for modelled prevalence.
